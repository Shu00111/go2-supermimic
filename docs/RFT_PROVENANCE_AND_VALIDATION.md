# RFT provenance and validation

## Authoritative source

The validated soil implementation is recovered from the separate repository
`https://github.com/Shu00111/Go2-LunarSoftTerrain`, not from a policy or an
unverified spring-damper approximation.

- Authoritative branch: `master`.
- Validated repository snapshot: `08568c617ed5c40a4b8969b09fa4b9b847772b85`
  (`Merge validated paper results and corresponding code`).
- NumPy reference implementation: `src/terramechanics/{params,stress,foot_mesh,rft}.py`.
- MuJoCo CPU coupling reference: `src/terramechanics/coupling.py`.
- Batched implementation: `src/terramechanics/rft_torch.py`.
- Reference/parity tests: `tests/test_rft_*.py`, especially
  `tests/test_rft_torch.py`.

The core NumPy RFT files reached their current source form at commit
`6297949f44f795b724378fffdd99a61368b188b7`; the batched Torch port and its
NumPy parity tests were introduced at
`59830a34b101044778349a36c0bbcc40dbda1d20` and revised at
`56cfe10f67f6995ab9b748b7c73450cabe0a3916`. The authoritative reproducible
snapshot remains the later validated `master` SHA above.

No SCMA, SG-v2, PPO, or other policy from the source repository is imported as
the new method. Only the soil equations, their state semantics, and validation
fixtures are eligible for reuse.

## Physics contract recovered so far

- Go2 foot geometry is a sphere of radius 0.022 m.
- Physics integration step is 0.002 s.
- Forces and torques are world-frame SI values (N and N m).
- Penetration is surface height minus the lowest point of the spherical foot.
- Force integrates depth-dependent normal capacity and tangential resistance
  over the immersed spherical surface.
- The elasto-plastic loading memory is independent for every environment and
  every foot. It records maximum penetration and the horizontal position of
  that record, resets at lift-off, and starts a new record after moving beyond
  the local contact width.
- Soil resistance is applied as an external foot wrench. The deformable ground
  must not also provide rigid foot contact, otherwise support is double-counted.
- Gravity remains the simulator's Earth gravity; it is not folded into the
  applied soil wrench.
- Soil strength uses the source coefficient scaling semantics. The soft-terrain
  configuration will expose this as a reproducible per-environment strength
  scale rather than silently changing the constitutive law.

## Implemented parity gates

`tests/test_soft_rft.py` contains fixtures generated by the authoritative NumPy
implementation at the SHA above. The tests cover CPU float64 parity, CUDA
float32 parity, release above the surface, loading-memory isolation, and linear
soil-strength scaling. The whole repository currently passes 29 tests.

An eight-environment CUDA integration run completed 100 policy steps (1,000
physics steps per environment) with finite states. Mean soil-force magnitude
was 36.7 N and mean sinkage was 0.0197 m. Uniform-soft foot geoms compile with
`contype=conaffinity=0`, so MuJoCo cannot add a rigid ground reaction to RFT.

For the hard/soft transition, a separate collision bit connects foot geoms only
to the rigid half-plane. A mixed-side CUDA test found 13 soft-side and 19
hard-side feet: the maximum hard-side RFT force was exactly 0 N, while 16 rigid
contacts were observed during the rollout. Thus the two support mechanisms are
mutually exclusive at the boundary.
